## Responsible Disclosure

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, please do not report it through a public channel like the issue tracker or social media. You should privately disclose your findings in an email using the following format:

To: `sappyduck@proton.me`  
Subject: `[SECURITY]: <ProjectName>`  
Body:
```
<Affected version(s)>
<Description of the problem>
<Steps or code that reproduces it>
<Your name and contact information, leave blank to remain anonymous>
```

> [!TIP]
> A rotating public PGP key can be used to encrypt messages sent to me through unencrypted channels, and can be found updated [here](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sappyduckie/.github/refs/heads/main/pubkey.asc). This key is additionally hosted at [keys.openpgp.org](https://keys.openpgp.org) under my email.

Barring extenuating circumstances, I will acknowledge your report within **14** days after receiving it, and will work with you to resolve the issue. The issue may be disclosed publicly **30** days following resolution, or **90** days following the initial report if there was not resolution in the **90** days.

Unless blank or otherwise specified, the name you provided in the initial report will be credited for the discovery upon public disclosure.

> [!WARNING]
> In alignment with the [AI_USAGE.md](AI_USAGE.md) policy, reports are to be written and reviewed by a human. AI may assist with or generate exploit code, but it may not assist or generate correspondence.
